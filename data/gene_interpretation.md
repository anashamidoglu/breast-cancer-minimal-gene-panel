# Gene-panel interpretation

The final 100-gene automatic panels share 10 genes with PAM50. Of their genes, 83 were selected in all five training folds. The 500-gene panel shares 31 PAM50 genes; 396 were selected in every fold.

These counts describe overlap and within-cohort selection consistency. They do not establish new biomarkers. Logistic coefficients describe conditional model associations, not biological causes. Aggregate coefficients and selection counts are available in the adjacent CSV/JSON files.
